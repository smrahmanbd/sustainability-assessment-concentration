Narration script: Sustainability Assessment Concentration video

Scene 1: Want to build a career in sustainability? Start here. Sustainability Assessment, an engineering concentration at KFUPM.

Scene 2: Four courses. One skill set that employers advertise for: manage, measure, report, and reduce environmental impact.

Scene 3: Course one. Be the engineer who keeps projects compliant. You will rank the environmental aspects of an engineering activity, run an internal audit, and scope an environmental impact assessment. And you will be ready for ISO 14001 auditor training.

Scene 4: Course two. Design buildings that use less, and earn a green rating. You will rate a building against LEED and Mostadam, propose design improvements, and back them with energy, water and daylight tools. And you will be ready for the LEED Green Associate examination.

Scene 5: Course three. Put a number on carbon, and prove it. You will compile a greenhouse gas inventory, quantify emission reductions, and plan a verification.

Scene 6: Course four. See the whole life of a product, then redesign it. You will carry out a life cycle assessment, calculate a product carbon footprint, and measure circularity.

Scene 7: Where can it take you? Employers in Saudi Arabia advertise roles such as environmental engineer, sustainability engineer, emissions analyst, and life cycle assessment analyst.

Scene 8: Assess. Improve. Lead. Ask your academic advisor how to enrol.
