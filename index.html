Sustainability Assessment

Want a career in sustainability? Four courses turn it into skills employers can check: audit an environmental management system, rate a green building, report a carbon inventory, and run a life cycle assessment.

See what you will be able to do
The concentration

What you will be able to do

Environmental Management and Assessment

Be the engineer who keeps projects compliant.

Build and audit an environmental management system, and scope an environmental impact assessment to Saudi national requirements.

You will be able to

  • Identify and rank the environmental aspects of an engineering activity
  • Plan and run a mock internal environmental management system audit
  • Prepare an environmental impact assessment scoping report with mitigation measures

What you will study

  • Environmental management systems and ISO 14001
  • Environmental aspects, impacts and significance
  • Saudi environmental law and compliance obligations
  • Performance evaluation and management system auditing
  • Environmental impact assessment: screening, scoping and baseline studies
  • Impact prediction, mitigation and monitoring plans

Standards covered

  • ISO 14001: environmental management systems
  • ISO 19011: auditing management systems
  • National Center for Environmental Compliance requirements

Who should take it

  • Civil and Environmental Engineering
  • Chemical Engineering
  • Petroleum Engineering
  • Industrial and Systems Engineering
  • Construction Engineering and Management

Professional pathway: ISO 14001 internal and lead auditor training.

Green and Sustainable Buildings

Design buildings that use less and earn a green rating.

Rate, design and improve buildings with the Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design (LEED) and Mostadam rating systems.

You will be able to

  • Evaluate a building against LEED and Mostadam credits
  • Propose integrated design improvements with cost in view
  • Use energy, water or daylight tools to support decisions

What you will study

  • Green building principles and rating systems
  • LEED and Mostadam certification process
  • Sustainable sites, location and transportation
  • Water efficiency in arid climates
  • Energy performance, renewables and commissioning
  • Materials, embodied carbon and indoor environmental quality

Standards covered

  • LEED green building rating system
  • Mostadam, the Saudi green building rating system
  • Saudi Building Code energy and green provisions

Who should take it

  • Architectural Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering (building services)
  • Electrical Engineering (lighting, power)
  • Construction Engineering and Management
  • Architecture

Professional pathway: LEED Green Associate examination.

Emissions Estimation and Reporting

Put a number on carbon, and prove it.

Quantify, report and verify greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions to international standards.

You will be able to

  • Compile a greenhouse gas inventory for an organization
  • Quantify project emission reductions against a baseline
  • Plan the verification of a greenhouse gas statement

What you will study

  • Greenhouse gas accounting principles and boundaries
  • Direct emissions: combustion, process and fugitive
  • Purchased energy and value chain emissions
  • Emission factors, data quality and uncertainty
  • Project-level reductions and removals
  • Reporting, verification and validation

Standards covered

  • ISO 14064, Parts 1 to 3: quantification, reporting and verification
  • ISO 14065: validation and verification bodies
  • GHG Protocol corporate standards

Who should take it

  • Chemical Engineering
  • Mechanical and Energy Engineering
  • Petroleum Engineering
  • Civil and Environmental Engineering
  • Industrial and Systems Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering (power)

Professional pathway: greenhouse gas inventory quantifier and verifier roles.

Circularity and Life Cycle Assessment

See the whole life of a product, then redesign it.

Measure the life cycle impact, carbon footprint and circularity of what engineers build, using life cycle assessment (LCA).

You will be able to

  • Carry out a comparative life cycle assessment that conforms to ISO standards
  • Calculate a product carbon footprint
  • Measure and improve the circularity of a product or system

What you will study

  • Circular economy principles and business models
  • Measuring circularity and material flows
  • Life cycle assessment: goal, scope and inventory analysis
  • Impact assessment and interpretation
  • Product carbon footprints and environmental product declarations
  • LCA software and databases

Standards covered

  • ISO 14040 and ISO 14044: life cycle assessment
  • ISO 14067: carbon footprint of products
  • ISO 59000 series: circular economy

Who should take it

  • Civil and Environmental Engineering
  • Materials Science and Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Chemical Engineering
  • Industrial and Systems Engineering
  • Architectural Engineering

Professional pathway: life cycle assessment practitioner and carbon footprint analyst roles.
